# 0080. Tag: a value in an outlined rectangle, a button that takes it away, focus to the next one

- Status: Accepted (2026-09-29; proposed by the agent to the product owner, who did not object, for the taste of the wave's review; the rest a technical decision within Wave 5)
- Date: 2026-09-29
- Related: 0047, 0052, 0057, 0079; brief §9.1, §9.4; WCAG 2.5.8

## Context

Brief §9.4 lists Tag/Chip in Wave 5; the product owner asked for the multiselect's chosen values as tags in this wave (2026-09-29). Facts, verified on 2026-09-29 (Angular 22.2.0, Chromium 153):

- The APG has no pattern for a list of removable values. Native buttons make each remove button a Tab stop with Enter and Space; nothing native, in CDK or in Angular Aria moves focus when the tag it was on leaves the page. Aria's grid can hold a list of tags (its guide's "layout grid"), with arrows between them.
- A removed element that holds focus drops it to `<body>`, and screen reader users lose their place.
- The badge (ADR 0079) is a tinted rectangle; the clear button (ADR 0052) names itself with hidden words through `aria-labelledby`.
- `size.control.xs` is 28px and `size.target-min` 24px; a target under 24px passes WCAG 2.5.8 when a 24px circle on it meets no other target.
- A multiselect field of `control.height.sm` is 28px in compact density, so a 28px tag would make it taller.

## Decision

1. **`@avelune/ui/tag`** (layer components), `AveTag` (`<ave-tag>`): the value's words, its content; `size` `md` (default, 28px) or `sm` (24px, for fields); `removable` draws the remove button; `(remove)` asks the application to take the value away.
2. **Look (the agent's proposal, not objected to):** an outlined rectangle, `border.default` on `bg.surface`, `radius.sm`, `font.label-sm` in `fg.default`, 8px before the words; the words wrap and are never cut. The remove button sits at the end, 1px inside the border: 24px square in `md`, 20px in `sm` (under 24px, it passes 2.5.8 by spacing: the tag's words lie between two tags' buttons), with Lucide's `x` at 16px in `fg.muted`, the hover fill under the pointer, the kit's ring inside.
3. **Name:** the button is "Убрать" and the tag's words ("Убрать Ташкент"), through `aria-labelledby` over hidden words (the new `remove` message) and the words, so it follows the content.
4. **Focus after removal:** when a press on its button takes a tag away, focus goes to the remove button of the tag after it among its siblings, then of the one before, and otherwise stays where the application puts it. No keyboard of the kit's own: Tab, Enter and Space are native.
5. **Harness:** `AveTagHarness` (`@avelune/ui/tag/testing`): the words, whether it can be removed, the button's name, removing it.

## Alternatives considered

- **A pill:** the product owner chose the pill for the count and the rectangle for the badge; the tag keeps the rectangle and differs from the badge by its outline and its lack of colour.
- **Aria's grid for a list of tags:** one Tab stop and arrows, but a composite widget for a handful of values, and screen readers in application mode; a list of buttons is read in place.
- **Delete or Backspace on a tag:** a key only people who know it press; the button is visible.
- **One size:** 28px tags do not fit a compact small field.

## Consequences

- The multiselect's chips are `sm` tags, drawn by the multiselect beside its trigger (ADR 0057, addendum).
- The application removes the value in `(remove)`; a tag that stays keeps focus on its button.
