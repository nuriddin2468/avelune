# 0085. Tree: Angular Aria's tree from data, one chosen node, rows indented by level, a chevron at the start

- Status: Accepted (2026-09-29; the look proposed by the agent to the product owner, who did not object; the rest a technical decision within Wave 5)
- Date: 2026-09-29
- Related: 0033, 0072, 0084; brief §9.1, §9.4; WAI-ARIA APG "Tree View"

## Context

Brief §9.4 lists Tree in Wave 5 and §9.1 puts its behaviour in Angular Aria. Facts, verified on 2026-09-29 (Angular Aria 22.2.0, Chromium 153):

- Aria's `ngTree` holds `ngTreeItem`s; an item's children are in an `ng-template ngTreeItemGroup` it owns (`ownedBy`), which enters the page when the item first opens. Items find their parent through `parent` and are ordered by the DOM. Each item says `aria-level`, `aria-setsize` and `aria-posinset` itself, so the rows need not nest: a flat list in which each item's group follows it is a valid tree.
- Aria's keyboard is the APG's: Up and Down, Right opens or goes to the first child, Left closes or goes to the parent, Home and End, typeahead by `label`, Shift+* opens the siblings. With one choice and `selectionMode="explicit"`, Enter and Space choose; a click chooses and opens or closes. Items are focused by roving `tabindex`.
- A work system's trees are an organisation's departments and a register's folders: one chosen node shows its content beside the tree, so choosing on every arrow would load a page per key.
- The sidebar navigation marks the current row with `bg.active` and a 2px accent bar (ADR 0072, product owner, 2026-09-29).
- A focusable element that holds its children draws its focus ring around all of them.

## Decision

1. **`@avelune/ui/tree`** (layer composites), `AveTree<V>` (`<ave-tree>`): `label` (required, names the tree), `nodes` (`AveTreeNode<V>`: `value`, `label`, `icon`, `disabled`, `children`, `expanded`) and `selected` (a model, the chosen node's value). One chosen node, chosen explicitly.
2. **Markup:** a `ul` with Aria's tree and a flat `li` per shown node, each group's rows right after its node, so a row's focus ring is its own. A node opens when the data says `expanded`, when the chosen node is under it, and when the person opens it; its rows enter the page then.
3. **Look (the agent's proposal, not objected to):** rows of `control.height.md`, `radius.md`, 8px inline padding plus 24px (`space.6`) a level; a 16px chevron at the start of a node with children, pointing to the inline end while closed and down while open, turned on `timing.slide`; a leaf keeps the chevron's room, so words align; an optional 16px icon 8px before the words, which wrap; the hover fill; the chosen row as the sidebar navigation's current one, `bg.active` and a 2px accent bar at its inline start; a disabled row in `fg.disabled`. Forced colours: the bar in `Highlight`.
4. **Harness:** `AveTreeHarness` (`@avelune/ui/tree/testing`): the shown nodes with their levels, the chosen one, choosing, opening and closing a node.

## Alternatives considered

- **Choosing on focus (`selectionMode="follow"`):** every arrow would change what the page shows beside the tree.
- **Nested `ul role="group"` inside each `li`:** the focused item's ring would surround its whole open subtree.
- **Several chosen nodes (checkboxes):** a filter of many departments; later, with a need, through Aria's `multi`.
- **Projected nodes:** Aria's items need their parent group, which a tree drawn from data gives each row.
- **Animating a group open:** the rows enter the page as their node opens, so there is nothing to grow from; the chevron turns.

## Consequences

- The showcase gains a departments page (`/departments`, under administration): the tree beside the chosen department's card.
- A tree with thousands of nodes draws only the open ones; a server's tree gives a node's children when it opens, which waits for a need.
