# 0071. Tabs: Angular Aria's tabs, tabs declared as panels, an indicator that slides on `timing.slide`

- Status: Accepted (2026-09-29, technical decision within Wave 4)
- Date: 2026-09-29
- Related: 0002, 0031, 0033, 0045; brief §6.3, §9.1, §9.4; WAI-ARIA APG "Tabs"

## Context

Brief §9.1 puts tab behaviour in Angular Aria. Facts, verified on 2026-09-29 (Angular Aria 22.2.0, Chromium 153):

- Aria's `ngTabs` holds an `ngTabList` of `ngTab`s and the `ngTabPanel`s they open, matched by a string `value`. A tab injects its list (`TAB_LIST`), a panel its `ngTabs` (`TABS`); both registries order their items by the DOM. The list selects the tab that has focus (`selectionMode: 'follow'`, the APG's automatic activation), wraps, keeps disabled tabs focusable (`softDisabled`), and moves with the arrows, Home and End. A panel is `inert` while its tab is not selected; hiding it is the page's.
- Aria selects nothing until a tab is pressed: `selectedTab` starts `undefined`, and every panel stays hidden.
- A directive in projected content resolves its injector through the element that declares it, so a panel inside a component projected into `<ave-tabs>` finds the `ngTabs` on that host.
- Brief §6.3: the tabs indicator slides with a transform. ADR 0045 gave a part that slides to show a state `timing.slide` (120ms, 0 under reduced motion) instead of `duration.normal`, which would still slide under reduced motion.
- `border-width.selected` (2px) is the token for selected tabs; `fg.muted` for inactive tabs.

## Decision

1. **`@avelune/ui/tabs`** (layer composites): `AveTabs` (`<ave-tabs>`), which carries `ngTabs` as a host directive, with `label` (required, names the tab list) and `selected` (a model, the chosen tab's `value`); and `AveTab` (`<ave-tab>`), one tab and its panel: `value` and `label` (required), `icon`, `disabled`. The application declares each tab with its content; the kit draws the list from them.
2. **Behaviour:** Aria's, as above. When `selected` names no tab, the first enabled tab is chosen, so a panel always shows.
3. **Look:** a row of tabs over a `border.subtle` line, each `control.height.lg` tall with `control.padding-inline.sm`, 4px apart, in `font.label-md` and `fg.muted`; the pointer turns a tab `fg.default` with a `border.default` bar; the chosen tab is `fg.default` over a 2px (`border-width.selected`) `accent.bg` bar that covers the line; a disabled tab `fg.disabled`. Optional 16px icon 8px before the words. Labels never wrap: a list wider than its container scrolls sideways without a scrollbar (the arrows, a swipe or a trackpad reach every tab), and the focus ring is drawn inside the tab. The panel starts 16px under the line. Forced colours: the bar in `Highlight`.
4. **Indicator:** one bar for the list, moved by `translate` and `scale` from the chosen tab's measured box (a `ResizeObserver` keeps it there), sliding on `timing.slide` and `easing.standard` only after a tab was chosen; it jumps under reduced motion.
5. **Harness:** `AveTabsHarness` (`@avelune/ui/tabs/testing`): the list's name, the tabs, the chosen and the disabled ones, choosing one, the shown panel's text.

## Alternatives considered

- **Aria's directives on the application's elements:** each tab would need the kit's look through a directive of its own, and every page would repeat the list's markup.
- **Tabs as data with templates:** the content would move into `ng-template`s; `<ave-tab>` keeps it in place.
- **A bar under each tab:** simpler, but it cannot slide, and brief §6.3 asks it to.
- **Manual activation** (Enter or Space after the arrows): the APG keeps it for panels that load slowly; the kit's panels are in the page already.
- **A visible scrollbar:** a classic scrollbar sits between the tabs and their line.

## Consequences

- Every panel's content is created with the tabs; a page that fetches a panel's data does it when `selected` names that panel.
- The showcase's contract page divides its content into tabs.
