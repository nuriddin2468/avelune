# 0011. Colour generation in OKLCH and contrast maths

- Status: Accepted (2026-09-23, product-owner go-ahead); the accent fill of the generator addendum superseded by [0019](0019-accent-exact-brand-dark-text.md)
- Date: 2026-09-23
- Related: 0003, [audit.md](../audit.md)

## Context

Palettes must be perceptually even across hues, and every declared text/background and UI-boundary pair must pass WCAG 2.2 AA in both themes. That means 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large text and for UI component boundaries (1.4.11). `BRAND_ACCENT` is "propose", with the direction "inspired by Ubuntu". Ubuntu orange `#E95420` gives only 3.65:1 against white.

## Decision

- **Generation:** primitive scales (neutral 50–950, accent, info, success, warning, danger) are generated in **OKLCH** by a script in `packages/tokens/scripts`, using **`colorjs.io` 0.7.1**. Lightness steps are fixed per scale index across hues so that equal steps look equal; chroma is shaped per hue.
- **Neutrals** are tinted toward the accent hue with chroma 0.005–0.015 (brief §4.2; Yaru and libadwaita both tint neutrals).
- **Gamut:** every value is mapped into **sRGB** with the CSS Color 4 gamut-mapping algorithm and emitted as hex (8-digit where alpha is needed). Wide-gamut (P3) variants are out of scope for 0.x. Contrast is therefore computed on exactly the colour the browser paints.
- **Contrast metric:** **WCAG 2.x relative luminance**, the normative metric for AA audits. APCA Lc is computed and printed in the Foundations stories for information only, never as a gate.
- **Checked by machine:** `tools/tokens-check` evaluates every pair in `packages/tokens/contrast-pairs.json` in both themes and fails CI below threshold.
- **Brand accent rule:** if the brand colour fails 4.5:1 against its on-colour, `color.accent.bg` uses the nearest passing step of the generated scale. The exact brand colour stays available as `color.brand.mark` for non-text use only (logos, illustrations, data marks), and contrast-pairs forbids pairing it with text.
- The **proposed accent** is presented at the Foundations milestone for approval, together with the Yaru-style "suggested action" question in [audit.md](../audit.md) §3.

## Alternatives considered

- **HSL-based scales:** perceptually uneven; yellows and blues drift in apparent lightness. Rejected.
- **`culori`:** equally capable. `colorjs.io` was chosen because its authors edit the CSS Color specs and its gamut mapping matches CSS Color 4.
- **Emitting `oklch()` directly:** fine at our browser floor, but it adds no value while we stay in sRGB and complicates exact-value comparisons. Rejected for 0.x.
- **APCA as the gate:** not yet normative in WCAG. Rejected as a gate.

## Consequences

- Palette changes are regenerated by the script, never hand-edited, and the diff of generated values is reviewable.
- `function-disallowed-list` (brief §5.3) allows colour functions only in `packages/tokens` sources and output.

## Addendum: the generator (Phase 2, 2026-09-23)

Implementation: `packages/tokens/scripts/palette.ts` (pure functions), `palette.config.ts` (inputs), `generate-colors.ts` (CLI). The output, `packages/tokens/src/primitives.color.tokens.json`, is committed; `pnpm nx run tokens:colors` fails when it is not exactly what the config generates (a hand edit or a stale file), and `--update` rewrites it. Scripts are TypeScript run by Node (ADR 0015).

- **Twelve steps: 50–950 plus 850.** One ladder serves both themes, and the dark theme needs more levels at the dark end (sunken/canvas, surface, raised, and borders that stay visible on the raised level) than an even 11-step ladder gives.
- **The lightness ladder is anchored on contracts**, checked for every step of every scale on generation: 600 ≥ 4.5:1 on white, neutral 50 and neutral 100 (text on light surfaces, and white text on it); 500 ≥ 3:1 on the same (UI boundaries); 500 ≥ 3:1 and 400 ≥ 4.5:1 on neutral 850, 900 and 950 (dark). Values: 0.985, 0.965, 0.925, 0.855, 0.76, 0.62, 0.52, 0.445, 0.37, 0.31, 0.265, 0.225. Green is the binding hue: at equal OKLCH lightness it has the highest luminance.
- **Chroma is capped at the sRGB boundary for the exact lightness and hue** of the step (binary search), then the CSS Color 4 gamut mapping runs as a guard only. Mapping an out-of-gamut target with the CSS algorithm alone ends in a clip that shifts hue: orange at L 0.52 went from H 38 to H 32.7, visibly redder. The generator rejects any step whose lightness drifts more than 0.005 or whose hue drifts more than ΔH 0.004 (OKLab units) after 8-bit rounding.
- **The brand colour is exact at orange 500.** `#e95420` has L 0.641, within the 0.025 tolerance of the ladder's 0.62, and meets the 500 contracts. It gives 3.65:1 against white, so the nearest passing step for `color.accent.bg` is **orange 600** (`#b53700`, 5.98:1); `color.brand.mark` references orange 500. *(Superseded by ADR 0019: the accent fill is the exact brand colour with dark text.)*
- **Hues:** neutral 38 (the brand hue, peak chroma 0.010, fading to 0.0025 at 50 so the canvas does not look pink), orange 38, red 22 (danger: crimson rather than orange-red, so it stays apart from the accent at equal lightness), amber 72 (warning), green 150 (success), blue 255 (info). The generator enforces the neutral tint of brief §4.2: peak chroma 0.005–0.015, hue within 5° of the brand hue.
- **Alpha series** for backdrops and shadows: `neutral-alpha` (neutral 950) and `white-alpha`, at 4–80%. DTCG keeps the six-digit `hex` and a separate `alpha`; the CSS output uses eight-digit hex.
- **Source split:** colour primitives live in the generated file; hand-written primitives (spacing, radius, …) in `primitives.tokens.json` (ADR 0003's list, otherwise unchanged). Each token's `$description` records its measured OKLCH, so the generated diff is reviewable.

## Addendum: dark fills and the contrast check (Phase 2, 2026-09-23)

- **Dark-theme role fills use step 400 with neutral 950 text** (hover 300, active 200). Step 600, the light-theme fill, gives only 2.57:1 against the dark surface, below the 3:1 a checked checkbox or a switch track needs (WCAG 1.4.11). Step 400 gives at least 5.77:1 against every dark surface and 7.48:1 with its text. This is also how the dark theme reduces accent chroma (brief §4.2): a lighter, calmer fill instead of a saturated one.
- **State layers are 4% (hover) and 8% (pressed)**, over the canvas, surfaces and raised surfaces only. At 8% and 12%, `fg.subtle` fell to 3.94:1; `tools/tokens-check` found it.
- **Composites are rounded to 8 bits per channel**, as browsers paint. Unrounded, `fg.subtle` on the pressed layer over a raised dark surface computed 4.50:1 while axe measured the painted 4.49:1; with rounding the check caught it, and ladder step 400 moved from L 0.745 to 0.76.
- **The dark end of the chroma curve falls off steeply** (800–950: 0.72, 0.45, 0.34, 0.26 of the peak), so the tinted backgrounds of the dark theme stay calm.
- **`tools/tokens-check`** checks every pair of `packages/tokens/contrast-pairs.json` in both themes. A translucent background is composited over each surface in the pair's `over` list first (source-over in gamma-encoded sRGB, as browsers paint), and a translucent background without `over` is an error. `neverText` lists `color.brand.mark`, which no pair may use.
