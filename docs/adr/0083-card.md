# 0083. Card: a bordered surface without a shadow, a heading row with its end, a row of actions at its foot

- Status: Accepted (2026-09-29; proposed by the agent to the product owner, who did not object, for the taste of the wave's review; the rest a technical decision within Wave 5)
- Date: 2026-09-29
- Related: 0061, 0062, 0066, 0079; brief §8.1, §9.1, §9.4; GUIDELINES.md "Foundations"

## Context

Brief §9.4 lists Card in Wave 5; Wave 6's dashboards and settings pages are made of cards. Facts, verified on 2026-09-29 (Angular 22.2.0, Chromium 153):

- The showcase draws its surfaces by hand: the register, its export and its empty state on `bg.surface` with a `border.subtle` border and `radius.lg`; the settings card adds `elevation.raised`.
- Brief §8.1 warns against "the identical soft shadow on everything"; the kit's popups and dialogs take `elevation.popover` and `elevation.dialog`, which say "over the page" (ADR 0069).
- Emulated encapsulation styles a component's own elements only: a heading the application projects is styled by a component on that heading, as `aveAlertActions` styles its row (ADR 0061).
- A card's heading level depends on the page: an `h2` in a dashboard, an `h3` under a section.
- A signal query (`contentChild`) leaves an arrow in the class's metadata (a `forwardRef` in `propDecorators`) that only TestBed's overrides call; in a small file it alone breaks the 90% function coverage (ADR 0026).

## Decision

1. **`@avelune/ui/card`** (layer components): `AveCard` (`<ave-card>`), the surface, and three parts on the application's elements: `[aveCardTitle]` (its heading, any level), `[aveCardEnd]` (what stands at the end of the heading's row: a status, a menu) and `[aveCardFooter]` (a row of actions at its foot).
2. **Look (the agent's proposal, not objected to):** `bg.surface`, a `border.subtle` border, `radius.lg`, no shadow; 16px from its edge to its content, the border included (the Alert's rule, so its height keeps the 4px grid), and 16px between its parts. The title in `font.heading-sm`; the heading's row wraps, its end at the inline end of the first line; the footer's actions at the inline end, 8px apart, wrapping. The heading's row is drawn only when the card has a title or an end, which count themselves in with the card they are in (a token the card provides) rather than through a query.
3. **No role and no interaction of its own:** a card is a surface. A card that stands for a record links it from its title (`a[aveLink]` inside the heading); a list of cards is the application's `ul`.
4. **Harness:** `AveCardHarness` (`@avelune/ui/card/testing`): the title, the text, the footer's actions.

## Alternatives considered

- **A shadow, or a border and a shadow:** shadows stay with what floats over the page.
- **A `heading` input:** the level would be the kit's to guess, and a link inside it impossible.
- **A card that is a link or a button as a whole:** its text becomes one long name, and its own buttons would sit inside a link; a link in its title does the same job.
- **Sizes and padding options:** a new look is a new variant through an RFC.

## Consequences

- The showcase's settings screen is a page heading over cards, without its shadow.
- The patterns of Wave 6 compose cards; a card's content lays itself out.
