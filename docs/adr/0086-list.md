# 0086. List: records on one surface between lines, and rows that fade and open as they come and go

- Status: Accepted (2026-09-29; the look proposed by the agent to the product owner, who did not object; the motion settles ADR 0031's deferred list item; the rest a technical decision within Wave 5)
- Date: 2026-09-29
- Related: 0005, 0030, 0031, 0083, 0084; brief §6.3, §6.4, §6.5, §8.2, §9.4

## Context

Brief §9.4 lists List in Wave 5. Facts, verified on 2026-09-29 (Angular 22.2.0, Chromium 153):

- The showcase draws a list of records by hand: rows on one `bg.surface` between `border.subtle` lines, a record's words at the start, its facts and its menu at the end (`contracts.css`).
- Brief §6.3's catalog: a list item enters with a fade and an expand, a stagger of at most 5 items × 30ms (`timing.stagger`, 0 under reduced motion), on `duration.normal` and `easing.enter`; it leaves with a fade and a collapse on `duration.fast` and `easing.exit`. ADR 0031 left the item's expand to this wave: an expand is a height animation, which brief §6.4 allows the accordion alone, by grid rows (ADR 0084 gave it `timing.expand`, 0 under reduced motion).
- `animate.enter` and `animate.leave` work in a component's `host`, and Angular removes a leaving element after its animations end. A list's first rows arrive with the page and should not move (ADR 0045, 0071).
- `@keyframes` live in `motion.css` only (non-negotiable 3), and the Foundations page "Motion catalog" proves every class there in both motion modes.

## Decision

1. **`@avelune/ui/list`** (layer components): `AveList` (`<ave-list label>`), a `list` named by `label`, and `AveListItem` (`<ave-list-item>`), a `listitem` with `[aveListStart]` (an avatar or an icon), its content, and `[aveListEnd]` (facts, a status, a menu).
2. **Look (the agent's proposal, not objected to):** the list on `bg.surface` with a `border.subtle` border and `radius.lg`; items between `border.subtle` lines, 12px and 16px of padding, the start 12px before the content and level with its first line, the end 16px after it, at the top; the content's text wraps. A list without items draws its empty box as the application's empty state puts it.
3. **Motion:** two new catalog classes in `motion.css`. `ave-motion-list-enter`: a fade on `duration.normal` and the rows from `0fr` on `timing.expand`, both `easing.enter`, delayed by the item's place among those that came together (at most the fifth) × `timing.stagger`. `ave-motion-list-exit`: a fade on `duration.fast` and the rows to `0fr` on `timing.expand`, `easing.exit`. The rows' animation is the accordion's technique, so it stops under reduced motion with `timing.expand`, and the stagger with `timing.stagger`; the fades stay. The items a list holds as it first renders do not move.
4. **The catalog's proof** learns an entry of two animations: the Foundations page plays the list's classes and checks both, in both modes.
5. **Harness:** `AveListHarness` (`@avelune/ui/list/testing`): the list's name and its items' text.

## Alternatives considered

- **A `ul` the application writes, with a directive:** the kit's rows need their parts and their motion on each item; `<ave-list-item>` carries both.
- **Collapsing on `duration.fast`:** it would keep closing under reduced motion; `timing.expand` stops, and the fade leaves first.
- **Measuring heights for the expand:** the grid rows need none.
- **Selection (a listbox):** a list of records is read and acted on through each item's controls; a choice among many is a select or a tree.

## Consequences

- `motion.css` has two more classes and two more keyframes; the invariants accept their timings as tokens.
- The showcase's contract page lists its files, which people attach and remove, and its history.

## Addendum: the start and the end are centred on the content (2026-09-29)

Decision 2 put the start level with the content's first line and the end at its top; the component, its docs page and its stories centre both on the content, which for the usual two lines (a name and a department) puts a 32px avatar beside both. The Wave 5 visual review found the text and the build apart. The build stands: the start and the end are centred on the content. With many lines (the LongText story) they sit by its middle, which the product owner's review of the wave may change.
