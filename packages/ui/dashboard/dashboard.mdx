# Dashboard

`<ave-dashboard>` is a work system's overview: the page's heading and its actions, a row of key figures, and cards of what waits for the person, in up to three columns (ADR 0098).

```ts
import { AveDashboard, AveDashboardActions, AveDashboardMetric, AveDashboardWide } from '@avelune/ui/dashboard';
```

<Canvas of={DashboardStories.Default} />

## When to use

- For a product's or a department's home page: what is in force, what waits, what changed.

## When not to use

- For one list people search: the List page.
- For charts and data visualisation: out of the kit's scope. A figure over time is its value and a note.

## Anatomy

- The header: the page's `h1` and `description`, with `[aveDashboardActions]` at the row's end, which wrap under the heading on a narrow page.
- The key figures, `<ave-dashboard-metric label value note>`: what it counts, the number in `font.heading-xl` with tabular figures, and what it means, on a card's surface. They stand in a row that wraps, each at least 192px wide, a list named by `metricsLabel`.
- The cards, the rest of the content: one column, two from `container.md`, three from `container.lg`, 24px apart, each row as tall as its tallest card.
- `[aveDashboardWide]` on the element around a card that needs room: two columns from `container.md`.

```html
<ave-dashboard
  heading="Обзор"
  description="Договоры юридического департамента на сегодня"
  metricsLabel="Договоры в цифрах"
>
  <div aveDashboardActions><a aveButton variant="primary" routerLink="/contracts/new">Новый договор</a></div>
  @for (figure of figures; track figure.label) {
  <ave-dashboard-metric [label]="figure.label" [value]="figure.value" [note]="figure.note" />
  }
  <ave-card>
    <h2 aveCardTitle>Ждут согласования</h2>
    <p>ДК-2026/114 · Поставка серверного оборудования</p>
  </ave-card>
  <div aveDashboardWide>
    <ave-card>
      <h2 aveCardTitle>Подписаны недавно</h2>
      <p>ДК-2026/113 · Перевозка грузов</p>
    </ave-card>
  </div>
</ave-dashboard>
```

Write each figure as the one node of its `@for` or `@if`: Angular projects a block's content into the figures' place only when it has one node at its root (NG8011).

## Content

- A figure counts what people act on: "На согласовании 5", with a note that says what to do ("ждут согласующих"). Format the number for the locale (`aveNumberFormat`); 0 is news, and shows.
- A card lists the few records behind a figure, each a link to its page, with a Count of them at its title's end.
- Four figures at most, and six cards; more belong in the registers.

<Canvas of={DashboardStories.LongText} />

## Keyboard

The page adds no keys of its own: Tab moves through the actions, then each card's links in turn.

## Accessibility

- One `h1`; each card's title is an `h2`. A card that stands for a group of records is a region named by its title.
- The figures are a list, each an item that reads its label, its number and its note in order.

## Motion

None.

## Do and don't

- Do put the page's main action, such as "Новый договор", in the actions.
- Do give every card the same parts: a title, a count, the records, a link to the whole list.
- Don't draw charts from the tokens: the kit has no data visualisation.
