# FormField

`<ave-form-field>` gives a control its label, its hint and its error, and marks it when it needs a value. The control inside, such as `input[aveInput]`, takes the label's id and is described by the hint and the error (ADR 0040).

```ts
import { AveError, AveFormField, AveHint } from '@avelune/ui/form-field';
```

<Canvas of={FormFieldStories.Default} />

## When to use

- Around every Input in a form, and around every later control that takes one value (Select, Combobox, DatePicker, Textarea).

## When not to use

- Around a checkbox: a checkbox carries its own label (`label[aveChoice]`). A group of checkboxes gets a fieldset (Wave 2).
- For a search box next to its button: an input with `aria-label` is enough there.

## Anatomy

In one column, the width of its container:

1. **Label**, above the control, 8px from it. Required controls get an asterisk after the label. Explain it once above the form: "\* Required field".
2. **Control**, projected.
3. **Hint** (`[aveHint]`), 4px under the control: how to fill it in. Optional.
4. **Error** (`[aveError]`), 4px under the control, after the hint: what is wrong and how to fix it, with an icon.

```html
<ave-form-field label="Contract number">
  <input aveInput type="text" [formField]="contract.number" />
  <p aveHint>As written on the signed copy.</p>
  @if (contract.number().errors().length > 0) {
  <p aveError>Enter the contract number, for example ДК-2026/114.</p>
  }
</ave-form-field>
```

## States

<Canvas of={FormFieldStories.States} />

- **Required:** from the form binding (Signal Forms' `required`, Reactive Forms' `Validators.required`) or the control's `required` attribute. The asterisk is hidden from assistive technology; the control itself says that it is required.
- **Error:** with a form binding, the error shows once the control is invalid and touched: after the person left it, or after the form was submitted. Without a binding it shows whenever the template has it. The control is described by it, and it is `aria-invalid`.
- **Disabled:** the label dims with the control.

## Content

- **Labels** are short nouns: "Contract number", not "Please enter the contract number". Sentence case, no colon.
- **Hints** say what people need before they type: a format, a limit, where to find the value.
- **Errors** say what happened and how to fix it: "Enter the contract number, for example ДК-2026/114". Never "Invalid value" alone, never an apology.
- Long labels and errors wrap; they never truncate.

## Keyboard

The field adds none: Tab reaches the control. A click on the label focuses the control.

## Accessibility

- The label is a `<label for>` for the control: it names it, and a click on it focuses it.
- The hint and the error are linked with `aria-describedby`, the hint first. Ids the control already describes itself with are kept, first.
- The error is shown as text with an icon, never by colour alone; it is not announced as it appears, but read when the control is focused.

## Motion

None: the error appears at once.

## Do and don't

- Do mark required fields with the asterisk and explain it once, above the form.
- Do write one error per field, the one that helps most right now.
- Don't show an error before the person has left the field.
- Don't put the hint in the placeholder.
