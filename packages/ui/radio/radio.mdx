# Radio

`aveRadio` turns a native `<input type="radio">` into the kit's radio button, inside the `label[aveChoice]` of the checkbox. Radios of one `name` are one choice: both form APIs bind them, a click on a label chooses it, and the arrow keys move the choice (ADR 0044).

```ts
import { AveChoice } from '@avelune/ui/checkbox';
import { AveRadio } from '@avelune/ui/radio';
```

<Canvas of={RadioStories.Default} />

## When to use

- For one choice among 2 to 5 options that people should see side by side: delivery, signing method, priority.
- In a `fieldset[aveChoiceGroup]`, which asks the question and holds the hint and the error.

## When not to use

- For 6 to 15 options: use a select. For more, or an unknown number: a combobox with search (GUIDELINES.md).
- For yes or no: a checkbox, or a switch for a setting that applies at once.
- For several choices at once: checkboxes.

## Anatomy

A 16px circle with a 3:1 border, then the label, 8px apart, the circle on the middle of the first line. Checked, the circle fills with the accent and shows a white dot.

```html
<fieldset aveChoiceGroup legend="Delivery">
  <label aveChoice><input type="radio" aveRadio value="courier" [formField]="order.delivery" /> Courier</label>
  <label aveChoice><input type="radio" aveRadio value="pickup" [formField]="order.delivery" /> Pickup point</label>
</fieldset>
```

## States

<Canvas of={RadioStories.States} />

- **Checked:** the accent fill with a dot. **Invalid:** a red border; the group's error says why, in words and with an icon.
- **Disabled:** a flat fill without a border, a dimmed label. **Focus:** the kit's focus ring around the circle.

## Forms

<Canvas of={RadioStories.Forms} />

- **Signal Forms:** `[formField]` on every radio of the field, each with its `value`; Signal Forms gives them their `name`.
- **Reactive Forms:** `formControlName` (or `[formControl]` with a shared `name`) on every radio, each with its `value`.
- Required is said once, by the group (`radiogroup`), never on each radio, where ARIA does not allow it.

## Content

Options are short and parallel: "Courier", "Pickup point", "Post". Put the likeliest first; don't preselect a choice people must make themselves. Labels wrap; they never truncate.

<Canvas of={RadioStories.LongText} />

## Keyboard

Native, per the WAI-ARIA radio group pattern: Tab moves to the checked radio (or the first), the arrow keys move and choose, Space chooses the focused radio.

## Accessibility

- The label names each radio; the legend of its group names the question.
- In forced colours the circle, the dot and the fill take system colours.

## Motion

Colour only: the fill changes in `duration.instant`. The dot appears at once.

## Do and don't

- Do keep the options of one question in one group, under one legend.
- Do write each option so it reads well after the legend.
- Don't use radios for more than 5 options, or to trigger an action.
