# Popover

`<ave-popover>` is a button that opens a small panel of content beside it: filters, a person's card, a short form of one or two fields. The panel is a non-modal dialog; the page stays usable around it (ADR 0065).

```ts
import { AvePopover } from '@avelune/ui/popover';
```

<Canvas of={PopoverStories.Default} />

## When to use

- For a few controls or facts that belong to one place on the screen and are needed now and then: filters of a list, a rename, the details behind a name.

## When not to use

- For a list of actions: use a menu.
- For a few words about a control: use a tooltip.
- For a task that must be finished or cancelled before going on: use a dialog.
- For a long form or anything with its own navigation: use a drawer or a page.

## Anatomy

The button, drawn as the Menu's: a Button of its variant and size with a `chevron-down`, or an IconButton (`icon`) named and titled by `label`. The panel: the raised surface of every popup, `radius.lg`, the popover elevation, 4px under the button, 16px of padding, at least 256px and at most 480px wide, never wider than the viewport. An optional `heading` at its top names it; without one the button does. It opens from the button's start, or ends at its end without room, and is pushed inside the viewport when neither fits.

```html
<ave-popover label="Фильтры" heading="Фильтры" [(open)]="filtersOpen">
  <fieldset aveChoiceGroup legend="Статус">…</fieldset>
  <div><button aveButton type="button" variant="primary" (click)="apply()">Применить</button></div>
</ave-popover>
```

`open` is a model: bind it to close the panel from its content, after Apply or Save.

## Behaviour

| When                                       | The panel                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The button is pressed                      | opens, and focus goes to its first field (or `cdkFocusInitial`); to the panel itself when it has none |
| The button is pressed again                | closes                                                                                                |
| Escape                                     | closes, focus back on the button; a dialog around it stays                                            |
| A press outside, or focus moves on past it | closes, and focus stays where the person went                                                         |
| Its content sets `open` to false           | closes, focus back on the button                                                                      |

The panel follows its button in the focus order, so Tab moves from the button into it and on past it.

## Content

- Keep it short: a handful of fields or facts. Actions at the end, the primary one last.
- Long text wraps inside the panel; nothing truncates.

## Accessibility

- The button has `aria-haspopup="dialog"` and `aria-expanded`, and controls the panel while it is open.
- The panel is a non-modal `dialog`, named by its heading or by the button.
- The kit's focus ring shows on its fields, and around the panel when the panel itself has focus.
- Text keeps its contrast in both themes; in forced colours the panel keeps its edge.

## Motion

The popover entry of the motion catalog: `ave-motion-popover-enter` fades in from `motion.scale.enter` at the button, in `duration.normal`; `ave-motion-popover-exit` fades out in `duration.fast`. Under reduced motion it fades without scaling.

## Do and don't

- Do give a panel of controls a clear end: Apply, or Save and Cancel.
- Do put the popover's button next to what it changes.
- Don't open a popover from inside another popover.
- Don't put a whole form in a popover; use a drawer.
