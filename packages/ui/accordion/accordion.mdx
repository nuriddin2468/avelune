# Accordion

`<ave-accordion>` is headings one under another that show and hide their sections: a contract's terms, the details of both parties (ADR 0084). It is Angular Aria's accordion, the WAI-ARIA accordion pattern.

```ts
import { AveAccordion, AveAccordionItem } from '@avelune/ui/accordion';
```

<Canvas of={AccordionStories.Default} />

## When to use

- For sections of one page that people read one or two at a time, in any order, where showing all would make the page long: terms, details, answers.

## When not to use

- For sections people switch between side by side: use tabs.
- For steps in order: use a stepper.
- For content everyone needs: show it; hiding it costs a click each time.
- For a single "Show more": use a button that shows the rest in place.

## Anatomy

Items one under another between `border.subtle` lines. Each is a heading of the accordion's `level` (3 by default) holding a trigger as wide as the item, at least the large control's height, with the heading's words in `font.heading-sm` and a chevron at the inline end, turned over while open; under it the panel's content, 8px from its sides and 16px from the line below.

```html
<ave-accordion [level]="3">
  <ave-accordion-item heading="Штрафы и пени" [(expanded)]="fines">…</ave-accordion-item>
  <ave-accordion-item heading="Форс-мажор">…</ave-accordion-item>
</ave-accordion>
```

Several items may be open at once; with `[multiple]="false"`, opening one closes the others.

## States

- **Closed** and **open**, which `expanded` holds (a model).
- **Hover:** the hover fill on the trigger; **pressed:** the pressed fill.
- **Disabled:** the words in the disabled colour; the heading keeps focus and does not open.
- **Focus:** the kit's ring around the trigger.

## Keyboard

Tab reaches each heading. Enter or Space opens and closes it; Down and Up move to the next and the previous heading, Home and End to the first and the last.

<Canvas of={AccordionStories.Keyboard} />

## Content

- The heading names the section in a few words; it wraps, and the chevron stays on its first line.
- A panel's content enters the page when it first opens and stays, so what people typed or scrolled stays too.

<Canvas of={AccordionStories.LongText} />

## Accessibility

- Each trigger is a button inside a heading, with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`; its panel is a region named by it, inert while closed.
- Choose `level` so the headings continue the page's outline.

## Motion

The panel opens from no height to its content's and closes back on `timing.expand` (200ms, `easing.standard`), and the chevron turns over on the same: the accordion entry of the motion catalog (brief §6.3), by the grid rows of brief §6.4. Only after the person has used the item; under reduced motion it opens and closes at once.

## Do and don't

- Do keep the headings short and distinct.
- Don't nest accordions.
- Don't put the only way to finish a task inside a closed section.
