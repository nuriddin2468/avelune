# Tabs

`<ave-tabs>` divides one page's content into sections that people switch between without leaving the page: a list of tabs over the panel of the chosen one. It is Angular Aria's tabs (the WAI-ARIA tabs pattern), each `<ave-tab>` a tab with its panel (ADR 0071).

```ts
import { AveTab, AveTabs } from '@avelune/ui/tabs';
```

<Canvas of={TabsStories.Default} />

## When to use

- For a few sections of one thing that people look at one at a time: a contract's facts, its approval, its files, its history.
- When the sections are of equal rank and none needs the others on screen.

## When not to use

- For moving between pages: use the sidebar navigation or links; tabs do not change the address.
- For steps that follow one another: use a stepper.
- For content people compare side by side, or read top to bottom: show it on the page, under headings.
- For more than about six sections: use a page per section.

## Anatomy

A row of tabs over a subtle line, then the chosen tab's panel 16px under it. Each tab is 40px tall (36px in compact density), its words in the label font; an optional 16px icon before them. The chosen tab's words take the text colour over a 2px accent bar that covers the line; the others are muted.

```html
<ave-tabs label="Разделы договора" [(selected)]="section">
  <ave-tab value="facts" label="Сведения">…</ave-tab>
  <ave-tab value="files" label="Файлы" icon="paperclip">…</ave-tab>
</ave-tabs>
```

`label` names the list for assistive technology. `selected` holds the chosen tab's `value`; while it names no tab, the first enabled tab is chosen. A panel's content enters the page when its tab is first chosen and stays, hidden, when another is. Register the icons with `provideAveIcons`.

## States

- **Chosen:** the text colour and the accent bar; one tab at a time.
- **Hover:** the text colour and a bar in the default border colour.
- **Focus:** the kit's ring, inside the tab.
- **Disabled:** the disabled colour; focus still reaches it, so people find it, but it cannot be chosen.

## Content

- Tab words are short nouns in sentence case: "Сведения", "Файлы", not verbs.
- Labels never wrap or truncate. When the tabs are wider than their container, the list scrolls sideways (the arrows, a swipe, a trackpad), and the chosen tab is scrolled into view. The tabs take their container's width and never widen it, so a container that sizes to its content (an inline block, a flex item without a basis) gives them a width.

## Keyboard

Per the WAI-ARIA tabs pattern with automatic activation, from Angular Aria:

| Key          | Does                                                        |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tab          | moves into the list, onto the chosen tab; then to the panel |
| Left, Right  | moves to the tab before or after and chooses it (wraps)     |
| Home, End    | the first, the last tab                                     |
| Enter, Space | chooses the tab with focus                                  |

## Accessibility

- The list is a `tablist` named by `label`; each tab a `tab` that controls its `tabpanel`, which it names.
- Only the chosen tab is a Tab stop; the panel follows it in the focus order.
- A hidden panel is inert: out of the focus order and the accessibility tree.
- The muted words keep their contrast in both themes; in forced colours the bar takes the system's highlight colour.

## Motion

The bar slides to the chosen tab on `timing.slide` (120ms) and `easing.standard`, the catalog's tabs indicator; only after the person has used the tabs, never as the page opens. Under reduced motion it jumps. Hover colours change in `duration.instant`.

## Do and don't

- Do keep every tab's content independent: nothing on one panel should need another.
- Do keep the tabs in the same order on every record of a kind.
- Don't put tabs inside tabs.
- Don't hide the only way to act in a tab people may not open; put the main actions above the tabs.
