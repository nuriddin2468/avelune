# Dialog

`aveDialog` turns a native `<dialog>` into the kit's modal dialog: a short, focused task that blocks the page until it is done or dismissed. The browser makes the page inert, moves focus in and brings it back; the kit draws the backdrop and the panel, plays the catalog's motion and keeps the page still behind it (ADR 0066).

```ts
import { AveDialog, AveDialogActions } from '@avelune/ui/dialog';
```

<Canvas of={DialogStories.Default} />

## When to use

- For a short task or a decision that must be finished before going on: edit a few fields, choose export options (`docs/GUIDELINES.md`, "Dialog, drawer or page").

## When not to use

- To confirm a destructive action: use a confirmation, `dialog[aveConfirmDialog]`.
- For details or a form next to the list it belongs to: use a drawer.
- For anything long, anything people bookmark or share: use a page.
- For a notice: use an alert, a banner or a toast.

## Anatomy

The `<dialog>` is the backdrop itself, over the whole viewport in `color.bg.backdrop`. The panel sits in its middle, 16px from its edges at least: the raised surface with the dialog elevation, `radius.lg` and a subtle edge. In it, the `heading` (20/28) and a close button at the top, the content, and the actions (`aveDialogActions`) at the end under a rule, the primary one last. The content scrolls between the heading and the actions, which stay in view.

```html
<dialog aveDialog heading="Изменить контрагента" [(open)]="editing">
  <form id="counterparty" (submit)="save($event)">…</form>
  <div aveDialogActions>
    <button aveButton type="button" (click)="editing.set(false)">Отмена</button>
    <button aveButton type="submit" variant="primary" form="counterparty">Сохранить изменения</button>
  </div>
</dialog>
```

`open` is a model: set it to true to show the dialog, and to false to close it after an action.

## Sizes

<Canvas of={DialogStories.Small} />

| Size           | At most | For                                  |
| -------------- | ------- | ------------------------------------ |
| `sm`           | 480px   | a question with one or two fields    |
| `md` (default) | 640px   | a short form                         |
| `lg`           | 960px   | a form in two columns, a small table |

On a phone the panel takes the width of the screen less 16px on each side.

## Behaviour

| When                                   | The dialog                                                                        |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `open` becomes true                    | shows modally; focus goes to its first field, or to its content when that scrolls |
| Escape, the close button, the backdrop | plays its exit and closes; `open` becomes false                                   |
| It closes                              | focus returns to where it was before, usually the button that opened it           |
| It is open                             | the page behind is inert and does not scroll                                      |

The close button comes last in the focus order, after the actions, and is drawn at the top.

## Content

- The heading says what the task is, as a verb and its object: "Изменить контрагента".
- Keep it short; a dialog that needs to scroll is often a page.
- The actions name their result: "Сохранить изменения", never "OK".
- Long headings wrap beside the close button.

## Accessibility

- A native modal `dialog`, named by its heading; the page outside it is inert, so neither Tab nor a screen reader's virtual cursor leaves it.
- Controls inside that announce (a file upload, a searched list) are heard: the dialog gives them an announcer inside itself.
- The close button is named "Close" in the locale and shows it as a tooltip.
- The panel keeps an outline in forced colours.

## Motion

The dialog entry of the motion catalog: the backdrop fades in (`ave-motion-backdrop-enter`) and the panel fades in from `motion.scale.enter` (`ave-motion-dialog-enter`), in `duration.slow`; both reverse in `duration.normal` before the dialog closes. Under reduced motion they fade without scaling, in 150ms.

## Do and don't

- Do open a dialog only from what people just did.
- Do put the primary action last and name it.
- Don't open a dialog from a dialog; a confirmation is the one exception.
- Don't use a dialog for a long form or for content people come back to.
