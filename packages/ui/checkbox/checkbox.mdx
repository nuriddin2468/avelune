# Checkbox

`aveCheckbox` turns a native `<input type="checkbox">` into the kit's checkbox, and `aveChoice` a `<label>` into its label. The input stays native: both form APIs bind it, a click on the label toggles it, Space toggles it (ADR 0041).

```ts
import { AveCheckbox, AveChoice } from '@avelune/ui/checkbox';
```

<Canvas of={CheckboxStories.Default} />

## When to use

- For one choice that is on or off, and takes effect when the form is submitted: "Notify the counterparty by email".
- For several independent choices in a list: each is its own checkbox.
- For a confirmation that must be given before sending: "I confirm the data is correct", required.

## When not to use

- For a setting that takes effect at once: use a switch.
- For one choice among several: use radios in a `fieldset[aveChoiceGroup]` (up to 5 options) or a select.

## Anatomy

A 16px box with a 3:1 border, then the label, 8px apart. The box sits on the middle of the first line of the label; long labels wrap under the text, not under the box. Every line of the label is clickable, and a single line is 24px tall, the minimum target size.

```html
<label aveChoice>
  <input type="checkbox" aveCheckbox [formField]="settings.notify" />
  Notify the counterparty by email
</label>
```

## States

<Canvas of={CheckboxStories.States} />

- **Checked:** the accent fill with a tick. **Mixed** (`indeterminate`): the accent fill with a bar, for a checkbox that stands for a group whose items are partly checked; a click clears it and checks the box.
- **Invalid:** a red border, with a form binding once touched. Say why in words: in a `fieldset[aveChoiceGroup]` the group's error does it, with its icon, and describes the group (ADR 0044). A checkbox outside a group needs its own message: point its `aria-describedby` at an element that is always in the page and holds the message while it shows, as below.
- **Disabled:** a flat fill without a border, a dimmed label.
- **Focus:** the kit's focus ring around the box.

## Forms

<Canvas of={CheckboxStories.Forms} />

- **Signal Forms:** `[formField]` on a boolean field; `required` in the schema means it must be checked.
- **Reactive Forms:** `[formControl]` or `formControlName`; `Validators.requiredTrue` means it must be checked.

## Content

Write the label as what happens when it is checked: "Notify the counterparty by email", not "Notification". Labels wrap; they never truncate.

<Canvas of={CheckboxStories.LongText} />

## Keyboard

Native, per the WAI-ARIA checkbox pattern: Tab moves to it, Space toggles it.

## Accessibility

- The label names the checkbox. A checkbox without a visible label, such as "select all rows" in a table header, needs `aria-label`.
- The mixed state is exposed as `aria-checked="mixed"` by the browser.
- In forced colours the box, the tick and the fill take system colours, so every state stays visible.

## Motion

Colour only: the fill changes in `duration.instant`. The tick appears at once.

## Do and don't

- Do phrase labels positively: "Notify the counterparty", not "Don't notify".
- Do use a required checkbox only for a real confirmation, with the reason next to it.
- Don't use a checkbox to trigger an action; use a button.
