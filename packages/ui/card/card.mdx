# Card

`<ave-card>` is a bordered surface for one record or one group of settings: its heading and what stands at the end of the heading's row, its content, and its actions at the foot (ADR 0083).

```ts
import { AveCard, AveCardEnd, AveCardFooter, AveCardTitle } from '@avelune/ui/card';
```

<Canvas of={CardStories.Default} />

## When to use

- For records shown side by side, in a grid on a dashboard or a board.
- For a group of settings or facts that belong together on a page.

## When not to use

- For many records to compare or sort: use a table, or a list.
- For a notice: use an alert. For something over the page: a popover or a dialog.
- Around every block of a page: space groups most things without a border (`docs/GUIDELINES.md`, "Spacing rhythm").

## Anatomy

The surface in `bg.surface` with a `border.subtle` border and `radius.lg`, no shadow; 16px of padding and 16px between its parts.

- `[aveCardTitle]` on the application's heading, at the page's level (`h2`, `h3`), in `font.heading-sm`.
- `[aveCardEnd]` on what stands at the end of the heading's row: a badge, a menu. It stays on the first line, at the inline end.
- The content, laid out by the application.
- `[aveCardFooter]` on a row of actions at the foot, at the inline end, 8px apart; the primary action last.

The heading's row is drawn only when the card has a title or an end.

```html
<ave-card>
  <h3 aveCardTitle>Поставка офисной мебели</h3>
  <ave-badge aveCardEnd variant="success">Подписан</ave-badge>
  <dl>…</dl>
  <div aveCardFooter>
    <button aveButton type="button">Продлить</button>
    <a aveButton variant="primary" routerLink="/contracts/114">Открыть договор</a>
  </div>
</ave-card>
```

## A card for a record

A card is a surface, not a control: it has no click of its own. A card that stands for a record links it from its title (`a[aveLink]` inside the heading), so the link's name is the record's and the card's buttons stay buttons. Put a list of cards in a `ul`, one card per `li`.

## Content

- The title names the record or the group, in a few words; it wraps.
- The footer holds the card's own actions; one primary at most.

<Canvas of={CardStories.LongText} />

## Accessibility

- The title is a real heading, so screen reader users move from card to card by headings.
- A card has no role; a list of cards is a list.
- In forced colours the border stays.

## Motion

None.

## Do and don't

- Do give every card of a grid the same parts in the same places.
- Don't nest cards in cards.
- Don't make the whole card clickable; link its title.
