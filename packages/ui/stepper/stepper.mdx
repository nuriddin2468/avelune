# Stepper

`<ave-stepper>` shows where a person, or a document, is in a sequence of steps: the steps done, the current one and those ahead, each state in a mark and in words (ADR 0077).

```ts
import { AveStepper, type AveStep } from '@avelune/ui/stepper';
```

<Canvas of={StepperStories.Default} />

## When to use

- Over a long form split into steps that people fill in turn: the steps done are a way back.
- For the stages a document passes through, such as an approval route, which nobody clicks.

## When not to use

- For sections people open in any order: use tabs.
- For a page's place in the product: use breadcrumbs.
- For a form of one screen: show it whole; steps add clicks.
- For the share of a long job that is done: use a progress bar.

## Anatomy

An ordered list named by `label`. Each step has a 24px mark and its words: its name, and a line under it. A done step's mark is the accent fill with a check, the current one's a 2px accent ring with its number, one ahead a ring with its number and muted words, one that needs attention the danger fill with a cross and its line in the danger colour. A line leads from each mark to the next, in the accent colour after a done step.

```html
<ave-stepper
  label="Оформление договора"
  selectable
  [steps]="steps"
  [current]="step()"
  (stepSelected)="step.set($event)"
/>
```

Steps are data (`AveStep`): `{ label, description?, error? }`. `current` is the index of the current step, from 0; the steps before it are done, those after it ahead.

## Orientation

`orientation="horizontal"` (default) shares the width among the steps, the words under the marks; in a container under 480px the steps go into a column. `orientation="vertical"` is always a column, the words beside the marks: for a route in a side panel or a tab.

## States

- **Done, current, ahead,** from `current`; the current step is `aria-current="step"`.
- **Needs attention** (`error`): a done step with an error in its fields, or a current one that was refused; say why in its description.
- **Selectable** (`selectable`): each done step is a button back to it, underlined under the pointer, with the kit's ring on focus. The current step and those ahead are not reachable from here.

## Content

- Name steps with nouns or short phrases, the same words as the step's heading.
- Keep to about five steps; group more into fewer.
- Long names wrap under or beside their mark; nothing is cut.

## Keyboard

With `selectable`, Tab reaches each done step's button and Enter or Space goes back to it. Otherwise the stepper is not in the focus order.

## Accessibility

- The list says how many steps there are, and the current step is announced as the current step.
- The marks carry the states in words: "Выполнен" and "Требует внимания" in the locale, not only a colour or a check.
- In forced colours the marks and lines take the system's colours; the current ring the highlight.

## Motion

None: a step's state changes at once.

## Do and don't

- Do keep the step's own heading on the page under the stepper.
- Do let people go back to a done step, and keep what they entered.
- Don't let people skip ahead: a step ahead needs the ones before it.
