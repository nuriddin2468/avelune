# Skeleton

`<ave-skeleton>` holds the place of content that is on its way: lines of text, a block for an image, a chart or a control. The page keeps its shape, so nothing jumps when the content arrives (ADR 0060).

```ts
import { AveSkeleton } from '@avelune/ui/skeleton';
```

<Canvas of={SkeletonStories.Default} />

## When to use

- While the content of a page or a region loads for the first time, and its shape is known: a list, a card, a form's values.

## When not to use

- For an action in progress (a save, a search): use a spinner, or a button's `loading`.
- For work whose progress is known: use a progress bar.
- For a wait shorter than 300ms: show nothing; a skeleton that flashes is worse than none.
- For content that failed to load: say so, with a way to try again.

## Anatomy

Parts in `color.bg.placeholder`, a colour of its own that stays visible on every surface in both themes. A band of `color.bg.placeholder-highlight` sweeps across each part from start to end (the shimmer); under reduced motion it stays out of sight and the fill stands still.

## Shapes

<Canvas of={SkeletonStories.Shapes} />

| Shape            | Draws                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `text` (default) | `lines` bars of body text: 12px tall in 20px lines, the width of the column, the last of several 60% wide                |
| `block`          | one box with `radius.md`, as tall as a medium control unless the page sizes the element (`block-size` in the page's CSS) |

Draw the skeleton of the content to come, not a generic one: as many rows as the list shows at first, the heading as a short line, an image as a block of its size.

## States

<Canvas of={SkeletonStories.Loading} />

The skeleton has one state: waiting. Replace it with the content in the same place when the content arrives. Compact density changes controls, not text, so text lines keep their height.

<Canvas of={SkeletonStories.Compact} />

## Accessibility

- The skeleton is hidden from assistive technology (`aria-hidden="true"`): it has nothing to say.
- Mark the region that loads `aria-busy="true"` until its content is in, and say that it loads in words, in a `role="status"` line or with a spinner's label.
- In forced colours, where fills disappear, each part keeps an outline in the text colour.

## Motion

The band moves with `ave-motion-shimmer`, the skeleton entry of the motion catalog: `timing.shimmer-period` (1.4s), `linear`, forever. Under reduced motion the period is 0 and the skeleton is a still fill (ADR 0031).

## Do and don't

- Do match the skeleton to the layout that replaces it, row for row.
- Do keep headings and labels that are known as text, and draw only what is loading.
- Don't show a skeleton and a spinner for the same wait.
- Don't leave a skeleton on screen after the content failed; show the error.
