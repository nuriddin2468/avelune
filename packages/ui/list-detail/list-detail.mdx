# List–detail

`<ave-list-detail>` shows a list beside the record chosen in it: the page's heading over the list and the record, side by side from `container.md`. On a narrow page one of them shows, the list and then the record under a way back (ADR 0096).

```ts
import { AveListDetail, AveListDetailDetail, AveListDetailList } from '@avelune/ui/list-detail';
```

<Canvas of={ListDetailStories.Default} />

## When to use

- For a hierarchy or a short list whose records people read one at a time without leaving it: departments, folders, a mailbox.

## When not to use

- For a register people search and filter: use the List page, whose records open on their own pages.
- For a record people change at length: open it on its own page (a Form page).
- For a quick look at a record from a table: a drawer (GUIDELINES.md, "Dialog, drawer or page").

## Anatomy

- The page's `h1` from `heading`, and `description` muted under it.
- `[aveListDetailList]` on the application's list: a Tree, a List, a DataTable.
- `[aveListDetailDetail]` on the chosen record: a Card or a form, or an EmptyState while none is chosen.
- From `container.md` the list and the record stand side by side, 2 : 3, 24px apart, their tops level.
- Below it, the list shows until `detail` is true; then the record shows under a ghost "Назад к списку" button (`backLabel` to name it after the list: "Все подразделения").

```html
<ave-list-detail
  heading="Подразделения"
  description="Структура организации."
  [(detail)]="reading"
  (detailChange)="back($event)"
>
  <ave-tree
    aveListDetailList
    label="Подразделения"
    [nodes]="nodes"
    [(selected)]="selected"
    (selectedChange)="reading.set(true)"
  />
  <ave-card aveListDetailDetail>…</ave-card>
</ave-list-detail>
```

## Choosing and going back

Set `detail` to true when a record is chosen; wider than `container.md` it changes nothing. The way back sets it to false. A Tree tells of a new choice only, so clear its choice when the person goes back, and choosing the same node opens the record again.

## Keyboard

The page adds no keys of its own. On a narrow page:

| When                           | Focus                                          |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| A record is chosen in the list | Moves to "Назад к списку", over the record     |
| "Назад к списку" is pressed    | Returns to the element of the list that had it |

## Accessibility

- One `h1`, the page's; a record's heading is at level 2.
- The pane that does not show is `display: none`, out of the reading order, so screen readers read one of them.
- Focus follows the pane that shows (WCAG 2.4.3); `main`, the shell's, holds the page.

## Motion

None: the panes change at once.

## Do and don't

- Do give the record's place an empty state while nothing is chosen.
- Do name the way back after the list when the page has more than one: "Все подразделения".
- Don't put a second list inside the record: open its own page.
