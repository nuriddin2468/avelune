# Spinner

`<ave-spinner>` says that something the person asked for is on its way: a search, a save, a part of the page that loads. It appears only after 300ms of waiting and then stays at least 500ms, so a quick answer never flashes it and a spinner never blinks (ADR 0058).

```ts
import { AveSpinner } from '@avelune/ui/spinner';
```

<Canvas of={SpinnerStories.Default} />

## When to use

- While an action runs or a small part of the page loads and its duration is unknown.
- Next to the words that say what is loading, or inside the region that is busy.

## When not to use

- For content that is on its way and whose shape is known: use a skeleton, so the page does not jump when it arrives.
- For a button's own action: set the button's `loading`; it shows the same spinner in its place.
- For work whose progress is known (an upload): use a progress bar.
- For a whole page: show its skeleton.

## Anatomy

Lucide's `loader-circle`, turning, in the colour of the text around it. The spinner's box keeps its size before it shows and after it has gone, so nothing moves.

## Sizes

<Canvas of={SpinnerStories.Sizes} />

| Size           | Box | With                      |
| -------------- | --- | ------------------------- |
| `sm`           | 16  | 12 and 14px text, in rows |
| `md` (default) | 20  | 14px text on its own      |
| `lg`           | 24  | a panel that loads        |

## States

<Canvas of={SpinnerStories.Delay} />

- **Waiting, first 300ms:** nothing shows; the box is empty.
- **Shown:** the icon turns. Once shown, it stays at least 500ms, even if the wait ends sooner.
- **Done:** the box is empty again. Remove the spinner, or leave it with `loading` false.

Bind `loading` to the wait; its default is `true`, for a spinner that is removed when the wait ends. The delays are `timing.spinner-delay` and `timing.spinner-min-visible`, the same as Button's.

## In context

<Canvas of={SpinnerStories.InContext} />

Put the spinner at the start of the words that say what is loading, 8px before them. Mark the region that loads `aria-busy="true"` until its content is in.

## Accessibility

- While it shows, the spinner is a `progressbar` named by `label` ("Загрузка договоров"), or by "Loading…" in the application's locale (uz-Latn, uz-Cyrl, ru, en). Name what is loading when the words next to it do not.
- Before it shows and after it has gone, it is hidden from assistive technology.
- It takes the text colour, so in forced colours it is the system's text colour.

## Motion

The icon turns with `ave-motion-spin` (`timing.spin-period`, `linear`), the spinner entry of the motion catalog. It keeps turning under reduced motion: rotation is not movement (ADR 0031).

## Do and don't

- Do say what is loading, in the words next to the spinner or in its `label`.
- Do keep the spinner where the content will appear.
- Don't show more than one spinner for one wait.
- Don't use a spinner for a wait shorter than its delay; it never shows.
